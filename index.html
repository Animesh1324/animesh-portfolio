I started in the lab at Unidrug Innovative Pharma Technologies, running HPLC, GC, and LC systems on live batches, working with manufacturing teams on GMP compliance, and learning firsthand that a failed QC process is a patient safety issue — not just a paperwork problem.

Then I pivoted — not away from pharma, but deeper into it. I completed an ICD-10-CM certification backed by IIT Hyderabad, where I learned how clinical data flows through healthcare systems and how accurate documentation shapes revenue cycles, insurance reimbursement, and ultimately the commercial decisions pharma companies make.

That intersection — lab science, clinical data, and business strategy — is what I'm building a career around. I'm now pursuing my MBA in Pharmaceutical Management at IIHMR University (2025-27), developing frameworks for GTM Strategy, Market Access, KOL Engagement, and Lifecycle Management.

Molecule to Market — bridging laboratory science with commercial strategy. I understand the molecule and the market. Not as a metaphor. Literally.
